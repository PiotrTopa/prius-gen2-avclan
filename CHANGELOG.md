# Changelog

## 2026-10-22: first publication

- Protocol documentation for the Prius NHW20 (2004–2009) AVC-LAN, from captures taken
  between 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-19 on one car with the factory navigation multi-display:
  bus basics, devices and roll call, display messages, climate and energy-monitor frames,
  navigation-unit emulation, touch calibration, negative results and open questions.
- Hardware appendix describing the transceiver used for the captures.
- Six example capture excerpts in CSV.
