(SVG + PNG), generated by `draw_schematics.py` (`.venv-draw/bin/python draw_schematics.py`).

Sources:

  and `gateway/docs/avclan-phy.md` section "The built board is NOT the schematic above".

## Bus / MCU facts used in the notes

- GP0 = AVC RX (PIO), GP1 = AVC TX (PIO); GP1 HIGH = dominant, idles LOW.
- Polarity: dominant (Data+ above Data−) → OUT HIGH → firmware `pol hi`.
- Car bus (2026-10-14): common mode ≈ 2.6 V; idle white − brown ≈ +50 mV;
  dominant: brown ≈ −0.5 V, white ≈ +0.1 V vs idle (≈ 0.6 V differential).
- LM339 VCC, Q2 emitter and R6 share the RP2040's 5 V (USB 5 V). The original netlist also
  contains a DM13-5V buck module (U3); the doc says there is no separate supply for the PHY, so it is not drawn.

## Why these drawings differ from older ones

| # | Old drawing / doc said | Correct (these drawings) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | R1/R2 = **100 kΩ** (netlist, avclan-phy.md, BOM) | **10 kΩ** (measured 9.62 k / 9.87 k) |
| 2 | Comparator **unit 1** (IN− pin 4, IN+ pin 5, OUT pin 2) | **unit 2** (IN− pin 6, IN+ pin 7, OUT pin 1) |
| 3 | 470 kΩ hysteresis/feedback pin 1 → pin 7 present (on the board since rev 1, drawn in some sketches) | **removed** 2026-10-14; no feedback resistor |
| 4 | 220 kΩ bias pin 6 → **GND** (undocumented, the root-cause bug) | 220 kΩ pin 6 → **pin 3 / 5 V** |
| 5 | Old H1 silk/label: "Data −" on the top pin, "Data +" on the bottom pin (**swapped**) | H1 left "T" = WHITE = Data+, H1 right = BROWN = Data− |
| 6 | Data+ → IN− (pin 6), `pol lo` (2026-10-08 state) | Data+ → IN+ (pin 7), `pol hi` |
| 7 | 2 MΩ 3V3 → pin 7 (2026-10-08), then → pin 6 (2026-10-13) | **fitted by design** on pin 6 (user-confirmed 2026-10-22), drawn as R2M; its shift is small (≈ +3.5 mV in the car, +16 mV on the bench) next to the 220 kΩ bias |
| 8 | earlier drawings mixed "as built" and "recommended" driver values without saying which was fitted | driver values confirmed by the owner 2026-10-22 and drawn as built |
| 9 | `phy-as-built.png`: 12 k bleeder drawn upward to an unlabelled end | bleeder clearly Q2 collector → GND |
| 10 | Early rebuild diagrams: D2 with cathode toward Data− | D2 cathode at Q1 collector |
